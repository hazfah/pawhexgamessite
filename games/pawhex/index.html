Follow the clues to find the pets!

A cozy pet-themed logic puzzle: flip tiles on a hexagon grid and use the clues to work out where the hidden puppies and kittens are — no guessing needed.

Download on the App Store

iPhone and iPad · Free with ads · Requires iOS [MIN iOS VERSION]

How it plays

  • Puppies play in the mud. The darker the mud, the more puppies are on touching tiles.
  • Kittens leave paw prints. The more paw prints, the more kittens are on touching tiles.
  • Tap or swipe to flip a tile (careful not to scare a pet away!). Press and hold to offer a treat where you think a pet is hiding.
  • No two puppies touch and no two kittens touch — but a puppy and a kitten can be neighbours.

What's inside

  • 500 puzzles per mode, grouped into puzzle sets, across three game modes.
  • Relaxed: unlimited treats and hints, so mistakes are free — take your time.
  • Challenge: 3 treats and 3 hints per puzzle.
  • Expert: one treat per puzzle and no hints — choose carefully.
  • Hints that explain the logic, magic treats that reveal pets, and perfect clears that earn rewards.
  • Collect and unlock dozens of pet breeds in the pet gallery.
  • Pure logic — no timers, no lives to wait for, never punishing.
  • Free to play with a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. PawHex is free to play, with a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts or sign-ups.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After you install it, you can play without a connection (the banner ad needs one to show).

Why is there an ad?

A single small banner helps keep the game free. There are no pop-up or video ads.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad.

Does my progress sync between devices?

No — your progress is stored on the device you play on.
